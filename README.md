Just a guy that loves FPV and software. I make free, open source tools for pilots and their radios, and more.. 

- **[StickTime](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime)** - FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio ([try it in the browser](https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/))
- **[EdgeTX Flight Log](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx-flight-log)** - read your flight logs right on the radio
- **[Groundtrack](https://github.com/juricabi/groundtrack)** - my fork of [RomanLut's Android telemetry viewer](https://github.com/RomanLut/android-taranis-smartport-telemetry): live map and 3D terrain for FrSky, CRSF, Ghost, LTM and MAVLink
- **[LapTimer](https://github.com/juricabi/LapTimer)** - ESP32 + RX5808 lap timer with voice and a phone UI, based on [PhobosLT](https://github.com/phobos-/PhobosLT)
- **[FPV Analog Upscaler](https://github.com/juricabi/fpv-analog-upscaler)** - cleans up and upscales your analog DVR to 1080p with AI on the GPU
- **[ELRS / Crossfire WiFi Joystick](https://github.com/juricabi/ELRS-Crossfire-WiFi-Joystick-Windows)** - fly sims on Windows, no cable
- **[fast-lua](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms)** - my fork of [EdgeTX](https://github.com/EdgeTX/edgetx) with much faster Lua on color radios
- **[BT AT Terminal](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx-bt-at-terminal)** and **[GPS Plus Code Widget](https://github.com/juricabi/EdgeTX_GPS_Plus_Code_Widget)** - small EdgeTX tools
