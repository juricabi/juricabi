Just a guy that loves FPV and software. I make free, open source tools for pilots and their radios, and more.. 

- **[StickTime](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime)** - FPV sim that runs on your EdgeTX radio ([try it in the browser](https://juricabi.github.io/sticktime/), [video](https://youtu.be/BUOUPDHlp58))
- **[StickTime Pi](https://github.com/juricabi/sticktime-pi)** - turns a Raspberry Pi into a virtual quad: StickTime in your analog goggles (composite to a VTX) or over HDMI, flown with your own radio ([video](https://youtu.be/Khq31t_4pqc))
- **[EdgeTX Flight Log](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx-flight-log)** - read your flight logs right on the radio
- **[Groundtrack](https://github.com/juricabi/groundtrack)** - my fork of [RomanLut's Android telemetry viewer](https://github.com/RomanLut/android-taranis-smartport-telemetry): live map and 3D terrain for FrSky, CRSF, Ghost, LTM and MAVLink
- **[LapTimer](https://github.com/juricabi/LapTimer)** - ESP32 + RX5808 lap timer with voice and a phone UI, based on [PhobosLT](https://github.com/phobos-/PhobosLT)
- **[FPV Analog Upscaler](https://github.com/juricabi/fpv-analog-upscaler)** - cleans up and upscales your analog DVR to 1080p with AI on the GPU
- **ELRS / Crossfire WiFi Joystick** for **[Windows](https://github.com/juricabi/ELRS-Crossfire-WiFi-Joystick-Windows)** and **[Linux](https://github.com/juricabi/ELRS-Crossfire-WiFi-Joystick-Linux)** - fly sims with your radio over WiFi, no cable
- **[fast-lua](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx/releases/tag/fast-lua-20ms)** - my fork of [EdgeTX](https://github.com/EdgeTX/edgetx) with much faster Lua on color radios
- **[BT AT Terminal](https://github.com/juricabi/edgetx-bt-at-terminal)** and **[GPS Plus Code Widget](https://github.com/juricabi/EdgeTX_GPS_Plus_Code_Widget)** - small EdgeTX tools
- **[True-D V3 mod firmware](https://github.com/juricabi/true-d-v3-mod-firmware)** - unofficial firmware for the Furious FPV True-D V3 module: much faster diversity, auto-tuning to your VTX, ELRS backpack, and channel control from Orqa, HDZero and Fat Shark goggles
- **[C3HT Backpack firmware](https://github.com/juricabi/c3ht-backpack-firmware)** - my fork of [ExpressLRS Backpack](https://github.com/ExpressLRS/Backpack) for ESP32-C3 VRx backpacks with a head tracker on the board: channel control and wireless head tracking for Orqa, rapidFIRE, SteadyView, RX5808, Skyzone and Fusion
- **[juricabi's HDZero goggle firmware](https://github.com/juricabi/hdzero-goggle-juricabi)** - built on [ripples' firmware](https://github.com/LVTY-AI/hdzero-goggle): analog module bay control from the goggle, sound in the WiFi live stream, HDMI audio and a bigger Audio page (pre-release)

Everything here is free to use. I show them on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@juricabi), subscribe if you like it :)
